This neuro-social community platform implements comprehensive security measures to ensure a safe, inclusive, and supportive environment for all neurodivergent individuals and their allies.

- **Trigger Warning Detection**: Identification of sensitive topics requiring appropriate handling

- **Link Safety Validation**: Verification of external links for security and appropriateness

- **Markdown Security**: Validation of links and content for community safety
